Paper ["Mitigating Spatial Disparity in Urban Prediction Using Residual-Aware Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks: A Chicago Case Study."](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3701716.3717368) was presented at [The International Workshop on Spatio-Temporal Data Mining from the Web WebST'25](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3701716.3717374), held in conjunction with the [ACM on Web Conference WWW2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/proceedings/10.1145/3696410?tocHeading=heading43) in Sydney, Australia, and received the Best Paper Award.
