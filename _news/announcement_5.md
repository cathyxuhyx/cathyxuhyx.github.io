Hanyong and her collaborators' research ["Mitigating Spatial Disparity in Urban Prediction Using Residual-Aware Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks: A Chicago Case Study."](https://webst2025.netlify.app/accept/paper_14.pdf) was presented at [The International Workshop on Spatio-Temporal Data Mining from the Web WebST'25](https://webst2025.netlify.appg), held in conjunction with the [2025 ACM Web Conference](https://www2025.thewebconf.org) at Sydney, Australia, and received the Best Paper Award.
